Need to know

Before you head over.

Is the kitchen halal?

Yes. Oxford Social is listed as having a 100% halal kitchen, serving brunch and food into the evening.

When is brunch served?

Brunch is currently listed as running until 2pm. Check Instagram before travelling for the latest service times.

Is the venue 18+?

Yes. Oxford Social is listed as an 18+ venue, so bring valid photo ID.

Can I book the upstairs privately?

Yes. The private upstairs floor has its own bar, toilets, DJ set-up and room for up to 200 guests.
